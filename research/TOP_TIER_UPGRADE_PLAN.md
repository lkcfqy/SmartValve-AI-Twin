## What was accomplished

The project now has three physical datasets, explicit dataset roles, a frozen source-only candidate,
eight strong neural comparators, source-OOF selective prediction, an untouched independent-bearing
test, physical-unit bootstrap inference, an exact six-test family, component attribution, immutable
artifacts, and retained failure/correction records. All decisive experiments fit the local RTX 3080;
the research did not require H100/H200 hardware.

This is a substantial research asset. It also did what a valid prospective design must be allowed to
do: reject the hoped-for algorithmic hypothesis.

## Frozen gate and observed result

The primary method needed a confirmatory-positive worst-environment or selective-risk endpoint on at
least two datasets, with no materially harmful endpoint on the third. After Holm correction:

- Cranfield: 0/2 positive;
- UCI Hydraulic: 1/2 positive, selective risk only;
- Paderborn: 0/2 positive; and
- total: 1/6 positive, one positive dataset, no resolved material harm.

Therefore the multi-dataset gate is false. MatchDG also exceeds PIRL on Paderborn pooled macro F1,
and the frozen margin ablation is inactive. These facts jointly block a defensible “new superior
algorithm” narrative.

## Path A — publish the current evidence as an audit

### Thesis

> Promising source-only industrial diagnosis improvements can fail to replicate when target access,
> strong baselines, physical units, selective coverage, practical effects, and multiplicity are
> prospectively controlled.

### Contribution stack

1. Access-explicit P0/P1/P2 protocol accounting.
2. Three-rig source-only evaluation with strong baselines.
3. Dataset-level prospective Paderborn validation.
4. Physical-unit uncertainty rather than window-level pseudo-replication.
5. Complete selective-risk reporting, including zero-coverage groups and effective accuracy.
6. A six-test family showing one positive result and five null results without selective omission.
7. Byte-level component attribution and transparent structural reconciliation.
8. Deterministic artifacts and a retained negative-run chronology.

### Remaining work

- write the integrated manuscript rather than submitting the current scaffold;
- extend related work around prospective validation and publication of null ML results;
- add appendices, diagrams, and a clear estimand explanation;
- obtain advisor/statistician review and an independent reproduction attempt; and
- choose a reliability, measurement, reproducibility, or domain journal/track whose contribution
  standard matches this evidence.

This path may produce a strong field paper, but venue tier and acceptance cannot be promised.

## Path B — pursue a future top-tier algorithm paper

Paderborn is no longer untouched. Any method changed after EXP-417/419 must treat D0/D1/D2 as
development evidence and use a genuinely new D3 for prospective confirmation.

### Required scientific change

The next candidate should address failures demonstrated here rather than add another lightly tuned
regularizer. At minimum it needs:

- a mechanism that remains active under optimization, unlike the frozen margin;
- source-selection criteria aligned with physical worst-setting and selective-risk endpoints;
- robustness to bearing-identity weighting, not only unweighted fold averages;
- an abstention objective that avoids zero-coverage target groups while preserving effective
  accuracy; and
- a clear advantage over MatchDG and other strong baselines, not only over ERM.

### Required validation change

1. Specify the new hypothesis and minimal effect before D3 access.
2. Freeze code, hyperparameters, seeds, endpoints, resampling unit, and family.
3. Choose D3 with multiple independent physical assets and lawful reproducible access.
4. Run one outcome-blind manifest and one sealed evaluation.
5. Accept either confirmation or rejection without endpoint substitution.

An additional random seed, larger sweep, alternative Paderborn endpoint, or favorable compound-fault
analysis is not a new prospective validation dataset.

## Compute strategy

The current feature-level MLP and baseline suite fit comfortably on the RTX 3080 10 GiB. EXP-434B
completed 720 additional 300-epoch fits in 1,767 seconds with a maximum method-level allocation of
about 151 MB. A future
method should first establish its scientific effect with compact models and cached physical-unit
features. Raw-signal encoders can be added as an architecture sensitivity analysis. Expensive
hardware is not the present blocker; the blocker is a replicated effect and a fresh physical test.

## Final decision

- Continue polishing the current work only under the mixed-result audit thesis.
- Do not submit it as a PIRL-superiority/top-tier algorithm paper.
- Do not tune on D2 and relabel D2 prospective.
- If a new algorithmic claim is desired, open a separate v0.4 plan with a new method and sealed D3.

evaluation cells fixed, the project now compares four access protocols:

- measurement-random, where identity and setting are shared;

paired bearing-bootstrap random-minus-crossed intervals exclude zero.

## 2026-08-19 sealed HUST result and source-volume falsification

The D3 step is no longer pending. EXP-445 completed the signal-unopened HUST evaluation and
EXP-447 independently recomputed it without refitting. All nine methods lost `0.226030--0.253205`
record-level macro F1 from recording-random to strict crossed access, all nine physical-bearing
intervals excluded zero, and the predeclared magnitude/count rule passed 9/9. Recording-random and
load-only performance was exactly `1.000000` for every method, so the frozen rank rule passed only
mechanically: Kendall tau is undefined under a complete tie and HUST does not reproduce
Paderborn's directional winner reversal.

The outcome-blind EXP-449 equal-source-volume control then fixed both arms at 24 source recordings
per fold while matching class balance, environment support, target recordings, and pair budgets.
All nine shared-minus-crossed effects remained positive, their median was `0.226030`, and all nine
intervals excluded zero. EXP-450B reproduced every artifact without fitting. Source-record count is
therefore not a sufficient explanation, although source membership/access still differ and no
causal leakage amount is identified.

This closes the most important empirical replication and quantity-confounding gates. The immediate
critical path is now: finish and validate motor-current plus paired sensor attribution; add a lawful
clean-room representation sensitivity or retain the limitation; generate deterministic paper
figures/tables; run clean-environment quality gates; and obtain human domain/statistical review.
